# Physics Labs — Upper Secondary School (Gy25)

A collection of physics laboratory materials designed and used in my teaching at Swedish upper secondary schools (gymnasium). During the 2018–2019 academic year, **multiple laboratory sessions** were conducted with my students at Örebro University's physics facilities, in formal collaboration with **Prof. Emeritus Dag Stranneby**. The lab below is a representative example of that work — additional labs will be added to this repository over time.

## 🔌 Featured Lab — Resistors and Ohm's Law (Physics 1)

📄 **[Read the lab document (PDF)](./Lab_Resistors_Ohms_Law_Physics1.pdf)**

Conducted at **Örebro University** in formal collaboration with **Prof. Emeritus Dag Stranneby**, Department of Science and Technology — Professor of Electronics Production and Director of Campus Alfred Nobel in Karlskoga.

This is **one of several labs** conducted during the 2018–2019 collaboration. Other labs covered topics such as:
- Mechanical forces
- Electrical circuits and resistances
- Interference and resonance
- Boolean functions

### Topics covered in this lab
- Resistance measurement with multimeter
- Series and parallel resistor configurations
- Verification of Ohm's Law (V = IR)
- Components that do *not* obey Ohm's Law (LEDs)
- Mathematical modeling: R = ρL/A

### Pedagogical approach
The lab bridges mathematical modeling, simulation, and concrete experimental verification — designed to help upper secondary students connect theory with hands-on practice.
