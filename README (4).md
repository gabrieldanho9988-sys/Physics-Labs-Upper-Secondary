# Physics Labs — Upper Secondary School (Gy25)

A collection of physics laboratory materials used in my teaching at Swedish upper secondary schools (gymnasium). During the 2018–2019 academic year, **multiple laboratory sessions** were conducted with my students at Örebro University's physics facilities, in formal collaboration with **Prof. Emeritus Dag Stranneby**.

## My role
**Gabriel Danho** — Licensed Upper Secondary Teacher (Physics & Mathematics) | M.Sc. Candidate in AI and Automation

I was responsible for:
- Bringing my upper secondary students to Örebro University's physics labs
- Guiding and supervising the students throughout the lab sessions
- Adapting the pedagogical delivery to upper secondary level
- Adding visual illustrations of the lab equipment to the lab document for clearer student understanding

## 🔌 Featured Lab — Resistors and Ohm's Law (Physics 1)

📄 **[Read the lab document (PDF)](./Lab_Resistors_Ohms_Law_Physics1.pdf)**

> **Lab author:** Prof. Emeritus Dag Stranneby (Örebro University, Department of Science and Technology) — Professor of Electronics Production and Director of Campus Alfred Nobel in Karlskoga.
>
> **Visual illustrations of equipment** added by Gabriel Danho to support student comprehension.

This is **one of several labs** conducted during the 2018–2019 collaboration. Other labs covered topics such as:
- Mechanical forces
- Electrical circuits and resistances
- Interference and resonance
- Boolean functions

### Topics covered in this lab
- Resistance measurement with multimeter
- Series and parallel resistor configurations
- Verification of Ohm's Law (V = IR)
- Components that do *not* obey Ohm's Law (LEDs)
- Mathematical modeling: R = ρL/A

### Pedagogical approach
The lab bridges mathematical modeling, simulation, and concrete experimental verification — designed to help upper secondary students connect theory with hands-on practice.

## 🤖 ML Labs — Machine Learning through Physics (Physics 1, in Swedish)

Two Python/Google Colab labs in which students take their own measurements and let the computer estimate the parameters of a model — the same idea as training a machine-learning model. Physics concepts (model, accuracy, precision, measurement uncertainty) are linked directly to machine-learning concepts (loss, training, training and test data).

| Lab | Physics | Mathematics and ML | Material |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ML-labb 1 – Hur exakt är π?** | circumference and diameter; accuracy, precision and measurement uncertainty | the model *C = k·d*, loss function, searching for the minimum, least squares, residuals | [Notebook](ML-labb-1_Hur-exakt-ar-pi/ML-labb-1_Hur-exakt-ar-pi.ipynb) · [Student guide (PDF)](ML-labb-1_Hur-exakt-ar-pi/ML-labb-1_Elevinstruktion.pdf) · [Open in Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/github/gabrieldanho9988-sys/Physics-Labs-Upper-Secondary/blob/main/ML-labb-1_Hur-exakt-ar-pi/ML-labb-1_Hur-exakt-ar-pi.ipynb) |
| **ML-labb 3 – Tyngdkraft, fjäderkraft och parameterskattning** | free fall (*g*) and spring constant (*k*), measured with a motion sensor | linear and quadratic fitting, mean squared error, loss curve, data cleaning, training vs. test data | [Notebook](ML-labb-3_Tyngdkraft-och-fjaderkraft/ML-labb-3_Tyngdkraft-och-fjaderkraft.ipynb) · [Student guide (PDF)](ML-labb-3_Tyngdkraft-och-fjaderkraft/ML-labb-3_Instruktion.pdf) · [Open in Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/github/gabrieldanho9988-sys/Physics-Labs-Upper-Secondary/blob/main/ML-labb-3_Tyngdkraft-och-fjaderkraft/ML-labb-3_Tyngdkraft-och-fjaderkraft.ipynb) |

The labs are a selection from a series in which physics topics end with an ML lab. Both notebooks run end to end in Google Colab; lab 3 includes built-in example data (`anvand_exempeldata`) for classes without a sensor.
